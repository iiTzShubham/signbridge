Tips for good samples
Use the ASL alphabet handshapes. For still letters, keep the letter shape while a recording runs but move your hand a little — closer, further, slightly tilted — so the model learns the shape and not one exact position. For J (little finger) and Z (index finger), each recording is one 2-second capture: start drawing the letter right after the countdown, at a normal, steady speed, and keep your hand in view. Samples from more than one person make it work for more people. Only the 21 hand-point numbers are saved (in trained_models/letters/samples.csv), never the camera images. ← Back to letter recognition
